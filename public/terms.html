Eurotrip terms of use

Eurotrip is a personal travel planning tool. It does not issue tickets or confirm reservations. Check dates, local times, station arrival requirements, travel documents, availability and booking conditions with the relevant operator. Imported screenshot text requires your review and does not verify a reservation.

Google Maps journey results are supplied by Google and are subject to the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Using this app's Google features means you agree to these terms. Results depend on provider coverage and may be incomplete or change. They are not a complete Eurostar timetable and do not establish pass eligibility or seat availability.

Use the app lawfully and keep credentials private. Live features require internet and may be unavailable. Keep separate trip backups. Review the privacy information before using live features or sharing trip information.
