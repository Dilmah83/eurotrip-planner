Eurotrip privacy

The native app stores your itinerary, bookings, selected documents and screenshot text on your device. Full backups and exported summaries contain personal information; share them only with intended recipients. Device backups follow your Apple settings. The browser app stores trip data in your browser.

Booking screenshots are read on-device using Apple Vision. Selecting a photo grants access to that selection; screenshots and recognised booking text are not uploaded for recognition. You review fields before saving. An optional compressed image is kept with the booking and included in full backups.

Live lookups send only the information required for the lookup through your configured Eurotrip server. For Eurostar journey searches this is the selected stations and departure time, sent to Google Routes. Google results are not saved in your trip. Nearby searches send a selected or rounded coordinate to the relevant map provider. Complete trip backups, attached documents and passport details are not sent with these lookups.

Server hosting and providers may process request metadata such as IP addresses and request times according to their policies. Your server access code and optional provider credentials are stored in the iPhone Keychain and excluded from trip exports. The Google Routes API key stays on the server.

Use of Google services is subject to the Google Privacy Policy. Other live provider information and limits appear in the app. You can delete local records in the app; remove backups or exports separately. Uninstalling the app removes its local trip files, subject to device backups; Keychain items may persist.
